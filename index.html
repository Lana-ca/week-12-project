A Rainy Day with Grandma Ruby

July 1985

I remember sitting on Grandma Ruby's porch on a rainy afternoon in July. The smell of wet earth mixed with the sweet scent of her peach cobbler cooling on the windowsill. She told me stories about her childhood, about how she and her best friend used to dance in the rain, their feet splashing in the puddles. Ruby was like sunshine, even on the cloudiest days. Her laughter could fill a whole room, warm and golden like honey dripping from a spoon. She always wore yellow – said it reminded her of hope and new beginnings. That day, she taught me how to listen to the rain. "Every drop has a story," she said, her eyes twinkling. And I believed her, because Ruby had a way of making the ordinary feel magical.
